        The inclusion $\horn{n}{i} \subseteq \Delta^n$ for $0 < i < n$ induces an inclusion\footnote{But not an embedding!} of simplicial categories
        \begin{equation*}
            \Ll(\horn{n}{i}) \to \Ll(\Delta^n)
        \end{equation*}
        with
        \begin{alignat*}{2}
            \obj \Ll(\horn{n}{i}) &= \obj \Ll(\Delta^n) &&= \left\{ 0, \ldots, n \right\} \\
            F_{\Ll(\horn{n}{i})}(j, k) &= F_{\Ll(n)}(j, k) &&= \Nn([n]_{j, k})
        \end{alignat*}
        except for
        \begin{equation*}
            F_{\Ll \horn{n}{i}}(0, n) = \bigcup_{o < j < n}\Nn(\left\{ \II \in [n]_{0,n} \mid j \in \II \right\}) \;\cup \bigcup_{\substack{o < j < n\\ i \neq j}}\Nn(\left\{ \II \in [n]_{0,n} \mid j \notin \II \right\}) \subseteq F_{\Ll(n)}(0, n)
        \end{equation*}

    \section{Proof of \ref{chIV-31}}
        We have to solve all extension problems
        \begin{equation*}
              \begin{tikzcd}
                \horn{n}{i} \arrow[r, "f"] \arrow[d, phantom, "\subseteq" rotate=-90] & \Nn^c \Cc \\
                \Delta^n \arrow[ru, dashed]           &        
            \end{tikzcd}
        \end{equation*}
        but by adjunction they are equivalent to
        \begin{equation*}
              \begin{tikzcd}
                \Ll(\horn{n}{i}) \arrow[r, "G"] \arrow[d, phantom, "\subseteq" rotate=-90] & \Cc \\
                \Ll(\Delta^n) \arrow[ru, dashed]           &        
            \end{tikzcd}
        \end{equation*}
        From Lemma \ref{chIV-33} it now follows that an extension of $g$ only has to defined on $F_{\Ll(\Delta^n)}(0,n)$. Furthermore, an arbitrary extension of
        \begin{equation*}
              \begin{tikzcd}
                F_{\Ll(\horn{n}{i})}(0, n) \arrow[r, "G_{*}"] \arrow[d, phantom, "\subseteq" rotate=-90] & F_{\Cc}(G(0), G(n)) \\
                F_{\Ll(\Delta^n)}(0, n) \arrow[ru, dashed]           &        
            \end{tikzcd}
        \end{equation*}
        will give a functor as desired since the only object mapping to $0$ is $0$ (and $F_{\Ll(\Delta^n)}(0, 0) = *$) and receiving a map from $n$ is $n$ (and similarly $F_{\Ll(\Delta^n)}(n, n) = *$), so there are no compatibilities with composition to be checked. But since $F_{Cc}(G(0), G(n))$ is assumed Kan a filler exists as the inclusion on the left is anodyne.\hfill$\Box$
